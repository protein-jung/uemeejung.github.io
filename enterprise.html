Concept Project

NeuroRoomie

NeuroRoomie is a concept project exploring how brainwave (EEG) data and spatial personality profiling (Space-BTI) can be combined to recommend interior layouts and styles tailored to individual emotional states.

Developed through startup bootcamps, this project envisions an ecosystem of personalized, neuroscience-driven space solutions — from diagnostics to real-time EEG-based consulting.

Product Vision

Two-Tier Model

Basic Model

Space-BTI

MBTI-style spatial diagnostics for personalized design recommendations. Questionnaire-based spatial personality profiling that maps individual preferences to interior configurations and styles.

Premium Model

EEG-Driven

EEG-based emotional response measurements for neuroscience-driven space solutions. Real-time brainwave data analysis to deliver hyper-personalized spatial recommendations aligned with individual cognitive states.

Diagnostic System

Space-BTI Framework

How It Works

This platform integrates Spatial Personality Profiling (Space-BTI) with a real-time brainwave database (EEG-DB) to deliver personalized solutions for interior styles, furniture layouts, and spatial configurations optimized for each individual's emotional state.

Sample Questions
Q1

When you enter a room, what makes you feel most comfortable? — A. Warm lighting and cozy textures  ·  B. Clean lines and open space  ·  C. Bold colors and unique objects

Q2

Which environment helps you focus best? — A. Quiet, enclosed spaces  ·  B. Bright, airy spaces with natural light  ·  C. Dynamic environments with flexible layouts

Result Types
S / P — Social vs. Private
B / C — Bright vs. Calm
A / R — Activity-oriented vs. Rest-oriented
S / F — Structured vs. Flexible
SBAS
Social, Bright, Activity-oriented, Structured — Open-plan, high-contrast, organized environments with bold visual energy.
PCRF
Private, Calm, Rest-oriented, Flexible — Soft lighting, cozy living room configurations with adaptable, muted aesthetics.
